FOUR DIRECTIONS - PLAYABLE PROTOTYPE

Windows: install Python with tkinter, extract this ZIP, then double-click Start_Windows.bat. First launch installs Pillow.
Or run: py -m pip install -r requirements.txt
Then: py game.py

Includes Chapter One choices, training, style unlocks, relationships, school and kit galleries, story save/load, simplified 11v11 matches with numbered bench and substitutions, and timeline export.

This is a starter prototype, not the complete game. Matches use a simplified seeded event engine; player movement is a tactical illustration rather than physical football AI. Nine squad players per school are placeholders. Relationship points unlock a status label; full romance routes and later story chapters are not yet written. Standalone campus and city backgrounds are not yet included: school group images provide the setting. Save stores story and completed results, not an in-progress match.

Story canon: Gun captained six equally skilled boys to nationals in their first middle-school year. All six then stopped playing. The reason remains undecided. Tadokoro and Midosuji were opponents, not members of that six.
